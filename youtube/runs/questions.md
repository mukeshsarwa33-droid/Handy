# Growth cycle: channel profile needed

The weekly growth cycle ran on 2026-11-09 but stopped before generating ideas.
`youtube/channel-profile.md` does not exist yet.

To run the cycle, please create `youtube/channel-profile.md` by copying
`.claude/skills/youtube-growth/channel-profile.template.md` and filling in every section:

- Channel name, URL, niche, core topic, upload frequency
- Audience now and the audience you want next
- Subscriber count and typical views (floor), best video (ceiling), if known
- Tone and stakes that fit the tone
- Off-limits topics
- What has worked and what has not
- Constraints (budget, equipment, people who can appear on camera, dates to avoid)

Optional but useful: export YouTube Studio analytics as CSVs into `youtube/analytics/`,
and add notes on outlier videos in `youtube/outliers/`.
